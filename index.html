About Me

Joshua Kim-Pearson

Hello I'm currently a student at the University of California, Santa Cruz studying for my B.S. in Computer Science: Computer Game Design. My main focuses are in game systems and mechanics, but I also persue projects in my free time to explore other areas of game development. I love creating games that are easy to pick up and play, but have depth and emergent complexity that can be discovered over time.

My primary hobbies include practicing and teaching taekwondo, military history, and playing videogames (primarily MMOs, and ARPGs). You may reach out to me through any of my contacts!
